## Style rules

- Put facts before interpretation.
- Every important number needs a source and date.
- If data may be stale, say so in the first half of the answer.
- If latest official disclosure was not found, avoid definitive language.
- Distinguish company facts from market-price judgment.
- Distinguish fund disclosed holdings from current real-time exposure.
